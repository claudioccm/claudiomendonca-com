title: A thinking partner that argues with your plans
description: A skill that makes Claude or ChatGPT poke holes in your idea instead of cheering it on. Install it once. It switches on when you ask it to.
audience: Owners weighing a big decision
triggers:
  - Poke holes in this
  - Challenge this
  - Play devil's advocate
  - Stress test this
  - Pre-mortem this
  - Is this a good idea?
steps:
  - "Start a new chat and describe the plan in a few lines: a new hire, a price change, a second van, a new service."
  - "Say \"poke holes in this\". It restates your idea in one line, lists what has to be true for it to work, and goes after the weakest part first."
  - "Push back where it's wrong. It won't fold just because you're firm, but it changes its mind when you give it a reason."
  - "Close to deciding? Ask for a pre-mortem. It pictures the plan failing six months from now and lists the likely reasons, with the early warning sign for each."
check: It argues so you think harder. It only knows what you tell it, and the call is yours.
