title: Follow up on quotes that went quiet
description: Most quotes don't get a no. They get silence. A short, no-pressure nudge brings a lot of them back.
category: Quotes & sales
audience: Trades and service businesses
prompts:
  - label: Paste, then list each quote under it
    text: |-
      Write a short follow-up from me, the owner of [BUSINESS] in [TOWN], to each person below who got a quote and hasn't answered.
      Use the job and the date I sent the quote. Two or three sentences, friendly, no pressure, no discount.
      Ask one easy question: are they still planning it, do they need changes, or should I close the file?
steps:
  - "Pull the quotes you sent in the last two months that never got an answer. For each one, note the first name, the job and the date you sent it."
  - "Paste the prompt above into your AI assistant and swap the brackets, with your list under it."
  - "Read each one, add a line only you could write, and send it the way you sent the quote. A week after the quote is a good time; a month is still fine."
  - "Mark who answered and what they said. \"Close the file\" is an answer too, and it clears your list."
check: Check every job name and date before it goes. A follow-up about the wrong job undoes the good the quote did.
