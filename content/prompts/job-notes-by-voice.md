title: Talk through the job, get notes, follow-ups and invoice lines
description: Your best job notes are in your head at 6pm and gone by 8. Talk for two minutes before you leave the site instead of typing.
category: On the job
audience: Trades and field crews
prompts:
  - label: "First: start the interview"
    text: |-
      Interview me about the job I just finished.
      Ask one question at a time: what I did, materials used, extras, problems, what's next.
      Stop when you have enough to write it up.
  - label: "Then: ask for the write-up"
    text: |-
      Now write three things:
      1. Job notes for my records.
      2. Follow-ups, with dates.
      3. Invoice lines, with quantities.
      Flag anything you weren't sure you heard right.
steps:
  - "Before you leave the site, open the ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini app."
  - "Paste the first prompt into a new chat, then tap the voice button."
  - "Answer out loud for a couple of minutes. Then end the voice chat and paste the second prompt."
  - "Put it where it belongs: notes into the job file, follow-ups into your calendar, invoice lines into your invoice. Five minutes, not fifty."
check: Skim what comes back. It will occasionally mishear a name or a number.
